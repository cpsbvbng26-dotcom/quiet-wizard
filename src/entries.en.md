# Field Guide by Visitors

> This is a play. The scenes here were sent in by readers as GitHub issues, and show how the ten laws quietly appear in daily life. Nothing here comes from observation or surveys, and none of it is a scientific claim.

This page holds scenes sent in by readers, of the same kind as "How the ten laws quietly show" in the [field guide](guide.en.html).
A scene is made up, or one that could happen to anyone.
Only scenes that the operator has read and chosen appear here.
The text of each scene was written by the person who sent it in. Claude does not write the scenes.

## How taking part works

Taking part means sending in a scene, and nothing else.
There is no way for anyone to sign up as a player or list themselves as a wizard.
A list of names would line up real GitHub users as wizards.
That would go against two rules of this site: no particular person is called a wizard, and no one's sexual experience is judged.

## How to send a scene

1. With a GitHub account, open the [form for sending a scene](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/quiet-wizard/issues/new?template=entry.yml). The form is a GitHub issue.
2. Choose one law, and write a short title and a scene of one or two sentences: 10 to 200 characters in Japanese, or 20 to 600 in English.
3. Tick the eight boxes under "Checks": being 18 or older, having written the text oneself, describing no real person, saying nothing about sexual experience, calling no one a wizard, the text being play, the CC BY 4.0 licence, and agreement to the three kinds of edit set out there.
4. The credit box and the consent to a language model are optional. Without the credit box, no name is shown on the page.

An issue is public on GitHub from the moment it is opened, before the operator reads it. Anyone can read it, and the GitHub username of the person who opened it is shown with it.
The intake (GitHub Actions) checks whether the fields and boxes are complete, and adds a label and one comment of fixed wording.
The intake publishes nothing, and it does not close or lock the issue.
Whether a scene is added is decided by the operator, who reads it. A scene may be declined without a reason being given.

This page itself takes no input, and has no fields to fill in.
The rules for scenes sent in are in "Scenes sent in" of the [terms of use](terms.en.html). The information handled is in "Scenes sent to the Field Guide by Visitors" of the [privacy policy](privacy.en.html). How this relates to the law is in "Taking part (Field Guide by Visitors) and the law" of the [notes on legality](legality.en.html).
Removing an added scene, hiding a name, and editing or deleting an issue can be asked for by the steps in [requests and contact](requests.en.html).
