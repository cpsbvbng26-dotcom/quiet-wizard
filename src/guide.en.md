# Stealth Wizard Field Guide

> This is a play. What follows is imagination: the ten laws of the canon applied to scenes from daily life. None of it comes from observation or surveys, nor is it a scientific claim.

## Appearance

- **Staff**: none. A folding umbrella instead.
- **Hat**: none. The umbrella is chosen to suit the weather.
- **Robe**: a navy or grey jacket, picked to last for years.
- **Familiar**: none. There is one potted plant.
- **Thermos**: yes. In Japanese it is a "magic flask", which is the only magic about it.

## Spells

The spells of a Stealth Wizard are short, and their effect cannot be seen.

| Spell | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Let me double-check | The talk pauses once, and there is one mistake fewer |
| I'll be heading off now | The wizard goes home. Nothing else happens |
| It's fine | It becomes fine. That can take a while |
| Just in case | There is one more spare |

## How the ten laws quietly show

| Law | How it quietly shows |
| --- | --- |
| Law 1 Establishment | Nothing happens on the thirtieth birthday. Nothing happening is the sign of establishment. |
| Law 2 Many Faces | In meetings the listening face is turned outward; among friends, the talking face. Both belong to the same element. |
| Law 3 Conservation | A word that went unsaid is not thrown away. It grows quietly at the back of a drawer. |
| Law 4 Reversal | Built-up frustration one day appears as a knack for noticing other people's frustration. |
| Law 5 Rule of Thumb | Some switch at twenty-seven. Counting years does not tell. |
| Law 6 Return | A result melts into someone's vague sense of "that helped, somehow". Sometimes even the wizard cannot see it. |
| Law 7 Common Practice | Those who have become one do not notice each other. If they do, they say nothing. |
| Law 8 Quagmire | After getting into bed, the brain runs one more lap. |
| Law 9 Secrecy | No specifics are written here. That is because of Law 9. |
| Law 10 Long Life | A long life. So knees and lower back get good care. |

## Belongings

- A spare charger
- Sticky notes
- A key of unknown purpose in the coin purse
- A notebook shown to no one

## How to tell one

There is no way to tell.
By Law 6, results cannot be seen from outside, and by Law 7, those who have become one do not say so to each other.
Using this guide to decide that someone nearby is a wizard is outside this play.
