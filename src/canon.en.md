# The Canon: On the Urban Legend of the Wizard at Thirty

> 〘本稿は論文とは別のものであり、著者の主体的な直観によるものである。〙
> In English: This piece is not a paper. It rests on the author's own intuition.

This page is a translation, not the original.
The original is a single Japanese text by the operator, and it stands next to each English rendering below, unchanged.
The English renderings were written by Claude. Where they differ from the Japanese, the Japanese is the canon.
They are not scientific laws.

## Definitions

| No. | Term | English rendering | Original |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Definition 1 | Wizard 〘魔法使い〙 | One who acts using both their own negative and their own positive, and produces results. | 〘自らの負と正の両方を用いて能動し、成果を出す者をいう。〙 |
| Definition 2 | Virgin 〘童貞〙 | One who has no sexual experience. | 〘性経験のない者をいう。〙 |
| Definition 3 | Action 〘能動〙 | Every kind of action. It is not limited to speaking out, recording, or reaching out. | 〘全ての能動をいう。発信、記録、働きかけに限らない。〙 |
| Definition 4 | Result 〘成果〙 | What arises from action. Its form is set by the time and the circumstances. | 〘能動によって生じるものをいう。その形は時と場合によって定まる。〙 |
| Definition 5 | Element 〘要素〙 | What makes up a being. An element is a regular polyhedron. | 〘ある者を構成するものをいう。要素は正多面体である。〙 |
| Definition 6 | Negative and positive 〘負と正〙 | The faces an element turns outward, depending on the time and the circumstances. The element itself is neither negative nor positive. | 〘要素が時と場合によって向ける面をいう。要素そのものに負と正の別はない。〙 |
| Definition 7 | Accumulation 〘蓄積〙 | The negative piling up without fading. | 〘負が消えずに溜まっていくことをいう。〙 |
| Definition 8 | Switch 〘切り替わり〙 | The accumulated negative beginning to reveal the opposite face as positive. | 〘蓄積した負が、逆の面を正として顕し始めることをいう。〙 |
| Definition 9 | Age thirty 〘三十歳〙 | A rule of thumb for the switch. | 〘切り替わりの目安をいう。〙 |

## Laws

| No. | Name | English rendering | Original |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Law 1 | Establishment 〘成立〙 | Anyone who is still a virgin at thirty becomes a wizard. | 〘三十歳まで童貞であれば、魔法使いになる。〙 |
| Law 2 | Many Faces 〘多面〙 | Elements are not fixed. Depending on the time and the circumstances, they change the face they turn. | 〘要素は固定されない。時と場合によって、向く面を変える。〙 |
| Law 3 | Conservation 〘保存〙 | The negative does not fade. It accumulates. | 〘負は消えない。蓄積する。〙 |
| Law 4 | Reversal 〘反転〙 | As the negative accumulates, the opposite face appears as positive. | 〘負の蓄積が進めば、逆の面が正として顕れる。〙 |
| Law 5 | Rule of Thumb 〘目安〙 | Thirty is only a rule of thumb. The switch can come before that age. | 〘三十歳は目安にすぎない。切り替わりは年齢に先立つことがある。〙 |
| Law 6 | Return 〘還元〙 | The moment a result appears, it returns to social relationships without a ripple. So it cannot be seen from outside. | 〘成果は、出た瞬間に社会的関係へ何事もなく還元される。ゆえに外からは見えない。〙 |
| Law 7 | Common Practice 〘共通〙 | Those who have become one all do this. | 〘成った者は、皆これを行っている。〙 |
| Law 8 | Quagmire 〘泥沼〙 | Once one becomes one, it is a quagmire. The brain does not stop. | 〘成れば泥沼である。脳は止まらない。〙 |
| Law 9 | Secrecy 〘秘匿〙 | Write down the specifics, and harm comes back to the one who wrote them. So the specifics are not written. | 〘具体を記せば、記した者に害が返る。ゆえに具体は記さない。〙 |
| Law 10 | Long Life 〘長命〙 | Becoming one means living long. Living long is hard. | 〘成ることは、長く生きることである。長く生きるのは大変である。〙 |

〘了〙 (End)
