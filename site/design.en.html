About this page

This page lists the visual parts of this site one by one.
For each part it records who made it, where it came from, what rights and licences apply, and which articles of Japanese law it may relate to.
The parts are the typeface, the seals, the favicon, the scene and the figures, the share card and the home-screen icon, the styles for colour and layout, and the script that draws the playground.
It records the operator's own understanding and is not legal advice to readers.
It has not been checked by a lawyer.
The statutes quoted are copied as they are from texts that a watcher in another of the operator's repositories retrieved from the e-Gov Laws API and recorded (see "Statutes" below). No statute text is written from memory.
Statutes and the phrases quoted from them are shown in the Japanese original. The English beside them is a summary, not a translation.
Laws outside Japan have not been looked into.
How the site as a whole relates to the law is in the notes on legality.

Typeface

Distributed typeface

Inter Tight is the only typeface distributed.
It is loaded through @font-face in style.css from a file on this site (assets/intertight.woff2).
Typefaces may be loaded only from this site (font-src 'self' in the CSP).
It holds 103 characters.
They are the 95 ASCII characters (U+0020 to U+007E) and 8 symbols (U+00B7, U+2013, U+2014, U+2018, U+2019, U+201C, U+201D, U+2026).
It contains no kana or kanji.
Japanese text is shown in the reader's own device fonts.
On the English pages, the Latin text of the body and headings is also set in Inter Tight. Japanese on the English pages (such as the originals of the canon and the Principia) is in the reader's own device fonts.

Text on the share card

The Latin text on the share card is set in Inter Tight and turned into pixels.
The card PNG is not a font file; it is an image made with the typeface.
The licence text says that the requirement to remain under the licence does not apply to documents created with the typeface (condition 5).

The requirement for fonts to remain under this license does not apply to any document created using the Font Software

The card carries no Japanese text, so that no Japanese typeface licence is brought into the card.

Redrawing the two PNGs

The two PNGs were drawn by node tools/card.js with Playwright's Chromium (version 153.0.8010.12).
Drawing them again with the same Chromium gives the same bytes; this has been confirmed.
With a different Chromium version, the bytes may differ.
So the check does not redraw them in a browser; it confirms the SHA-256 in the table above, and the size and colours of the PNGs.

Typefaces named only

The font stacks in style.css, and the settings in the figures for GitHub, name typefaces to use if the device has them.
This site does not distribute these typefaces' files. It only writes their names.

Named typefaces: Helvetica Neue, Arial, Hiragino Mincho ProN, Yu Mincho, YuMincho, Noto Serif JP, Noto Serif CJK JP, Hiragino Sans, Yu Gothic UI, Yu Gothic, Noto Sans JP.
Words that point to the device's default typefaces: system-ui, -apple-system, serif, sans-serif.

Materials loaded from outside

No materials are loaded from outside.
The pages request nothing from any other server (default-src 'none' in the CSP).
Typefaces, images, scripts and styles are all loaded from this site.

Copyright

Whether the design is a copyrighted work

The Copyright Act defines a work as 「思想又は感情を創作的に表現したもの」 (in summary: a creative expression of thoughts or feelings) that falls within literature, scholarship, fine art or music (Article 2(1)(i)).
Works of fine art are listed among the examples of works (Article 10(1)(iv)).
The seals, favicon, scene, figures, share card and icon of this site are shapes drawn by Claude at the operator's request.
Whether shapes drawn by a language model meet this definition is not settled by the wording of the statute.
This page decides neither that they do nor that they do not.
If any part does, that part may be used under the licences above.

Moral rights of the author

The right of attribution (Article 19) and the right to integrity (Article 20) are rights of the author.
This site does not treat Claude as an author.
The figures carry no author's name.

For the typeface, the name shown in its copyright line (The Inter Project Authors) is shown as it is on the disclaimers and disclosure page and in the licence text.
Article 19(2) says that a person using a work may show the author's name 「すでに著作者が表示しているところに従つて著作者名を表示することができる」 (in summary: as the author has already shown it).

Cutting the typeface down to Latin characters means removing part of it.
The licence text permits modifying the typeface.

to use, study, copy, merge, embed, modify, redistribute, and sell modified and unmodified copies of the Font Software

The licence text calls what is made by removing parts a Modified Version.

"Modified Version" refers to any derivative made by adding to, deleting, or substituting -- in part or in whole -- any of the components of the Original Version

Whether a typeface is a work under this Act is also left undecided by this page.

Conditions of the typeface licence

  • Not selling the typeface by itself (condition 1): this site sells nothing.
  • Including the copyright notice and the licence text (condition 2): the licence text is distributed as assets/OFL-InterTight.txt, and the name table of the font file keeps its copyright entry (nameID 0) and licence entry (nameID 13).
  • Reserved Font Names (condition 3): the copyright line of the licence text declares no Reserved Font Name. The cut-down copy keeps the name Inter Tight.
  • Distributing under the same licence (condition 5): the typeface is distributed under this licence only. It is placed under neither CC BY 4.0 nor MIT.

Other people's materials

Apart from the typeface, no material made by anyone else is used.
There is not a single photograph in the repository.
The raster images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, ICO and so on) are only the two PNGs: the share card (assets/card.png) and the home-screen icon (assets/apple-touch-icon.png). site/assets/ holds copies of them.
Both were drawn by tools/card.js from this repository's emblems, colours and typeface, and contain no images from elsewhere.
The SVGs are built only from the elements svg, g, path, polygon, circle, ellipse, rect, line and text.
They hold no embedded images and no references to other files.
The playground script draws only circle and line elements.

Trademarks, designs and unfair competition

Filing and registration

The names of the site (ステルス・ウィザード, 地味な魔法使い, quiet-wizard) have not been filed as trademarks ("Names" in the terms of use).
There is no record in this repository of the seals, favicon, scene, figures, share card or icon being filed as trademarks or designs.
Whether the same or similar names, or similar shapes, are registered as trademarks or designs has not been searched in the Japan Patent Office database.

Design Act

The Design Act includes images among designs.
But images are limited to those 「機器の操作の用に供されるもの又は機器がその機能を発揮した結果として表示されるもの」 (in summary: images used to operate a device, or shown as a result of a device performing its function) (Article 2(1)).
This page does not decide whether the scene or figures of this site fall under this.
A design right covers working the design 「業として」 (in summary: as a business) (Article 23).
This site sells nothing and charges no one.
Whether that is enough to say it is not "as a business" is also not settled by the wording of the statute.
No comparison with other people's registered designs has been made.

Trademark Act

A trademark holder 「指定商品又は指定役務について登録商標の使用をする権利を専有する」 (in summary: holds the exclusive right to use the registered trademark for the designated goods or services) (Article 25).
This site shows the name Inter Tight only as the name of a typeface.
It does not use it as the name of any goods or services of its own.
The name table of the font file (nameID 7) states that the name Inter is a trademark.
The other typeface names (see "Typefaces named only" above) are also written only so that the device can choose a typeface.

Unfair Competition Prevention Act

Article 2(1)(i) and (ii) list acts of using a mark identical or similar to someone else's 「商品等表示」 (in summary: indication of goods or business).
Item (iii) lists acts such as transferring, or providing over telecommunication lines, 「模倣した商品」 (in summary: goods that imitate) someone else's 「商品の形態」 (in summary: configuration of goods).
The name, seals, favicon, scene, figures, share card and icon of this site were not made by copying anyone else's marks or images.
As set out in "Other people's materials" above, they are drawn only from shape elements.
Whether the shapes drawn resemble someone else's well-known marks or product configurations has not been compared.
If a rights holder gets in touch, the part will be redrawn or no longer used (requests and contact).

Accessibility

The 障害を理由とする差別の解消の推進に関する法律（障害者差別解消法） (the Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities) defines a 「事業者」 (in summary: business operator) as 「商業その他の事業を行う者」 (in summary: a person who carries on commerce or other business) (Article 2(vii)).
It requires business operators not to engage in 「不当な差別的取扱い」 (in summary: unjust discriminatory treatment) on the ground of disability, and to provide 「必要かつ合理的な配慮」 (in summary: necessary and reasonable accommodation) for removing social barriers (Article 8).
Whether the operator is a "business operator" under this Act is not settled by the wording of the statute.
This page decides neither that it is nor that it is not.

With that said, here are the things this site does about its appearance that the check confirms.

  • The contrast ratio between the text colours (--ink, --muted, --accent, --gold) and the ground colours (--bg, --tint) is at least 4.5 in both the light and the dark scheme. The lowest is the light scheme's --muted against --tint, at 5.73.
  • The ratio between the header text colours (--mastink, --mastmuted, --mastgold) and its ground colour (--mastbg) is also at least 4.5. The header has the same colours in both schemes.
  • The ratio between the colours of the playground's lines and dots and the ground colours of the two views is at least 3. The lowest in the playground's network is the wizard's view's #5d6672 against the ground #12161c, at 3.12.
  • Text inside figures does not refer to shapes by colour names, because the line colours of the figures turn lighter in the dark scheme.
  • On the English pages, the text in the figures and the scene is replaced with English through the FIG_EN table in build.js. Each figure is still a single file. The English text also does not refer to shapes by colour names.
  • With the reduced-motion setting (prefers-reduced-motion), every animation in style.css stops, and so does the transition of the home page scene. "Let the days run" in the playground lengthens each day from 800 milliseconds to 1500 milliseconds.
  • Images in the pages carry alternative text. Each img has alt, the figures, scene and playground network have aria-label, and decorative SVGs are hidden from screen readers with aria-hidden. The share card has alternative text through og:image:alt.
  • Parts operated by keyboard draw a focus ring (a 2px line with :focus-visible).
  • Every page has a skip link ("Skip to content") at its start. It is visible only when selected by keyboard.
  • In print, the top band, the header and the class sheet are printed in black on white.
  • Zoom is not blocked. The viewport setting contains neither maximum-scale nor user-scalable.

The following have not been checked.

  • The contrast of colours written directly in the page (the top band, the class sheet, the scene).
  • The contrast where the soft background tints overlap.
  • Whether everything outside the figure text can be read without relying on colour alone.
  • How screen readers actually read the pages.

When something changes

When a part changes, this page and the check are corrected in the same change (the same PR).
The Japanese page and this English page are corrected together. The check compares this page's table, numbers and facts with the Japanese page.
Adding or removing a part file makes it disagree with the table above, and the check fails.
Replacing the typeface makes the SHA-256 of its contents disagree, and the check fails.
When this page changes, the last-updated date is revised. Earlier versions remain in the repository history.

２ 著作物を利用する者は、その著作者の別段の意思表示がない限り、その著作物につきすでに著作者が表示しているところに従つて著作者名を表示することができる。

Social barriers are things, systems, customs, ideas and anything else in society that act as barriers. A business operator is a person who carries on commerce or other business, other than the state, incorporated administrative agencies, local governments and local incorporated administrative agencies.

Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities, Article 8 （障害者差別解消法 第8条）

（事業者における障害を理由とする差別の禁止） 第八条 事業者は、その事業を行うに当たり、障害を理由として障害者でない者と不当な差別的取扱いをすることにより、障害者の権利利益を侵害してはならない。 ２ 事業者は、その事業を行うに当たり、障害者から現に社会的障壁の除去を必要としている旨の意思の表明があった場合において、その実施に伴う負担が過重でないときは、障害者の権利利益を侵害することとならないよう、当該障害者の性別、年齢及び障害の状態に応じて、社会的障壁の除去の実施について必要かつ合理的な配慮をしなければならない。

Summary, not a translationIn carrying on business, a business operator must not infringe the rights and interests of persons with disabilities by unjust discriminatory treatment on the ground of disability. When a person with a disability asks for a social barrier to be removed and the burden is not excessive, the operator must provide necessary and reasonable accommodation according to that person's sex, age and state of disability.
