Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions

問い

This is a play. The answers read the text of the canon as a game.

Is it science?

No.
This is an entertainment space, separate from the operator's academic work. It is not science.
The laws of the canon were written from intuition; no experiment or survey has tested them.
This page, too, is reading for fun.

Can a wizard really use magic?

A wizard on this site means only what Definition 1 of the canon says.
It is one who acts using both their own negative and their own positive, and produces results.
It has nothing to do with supernatural powers.

Are wizards better than other people?

No.
This site does not treat wizards as better than other people, or as chosen. It is not elitism.
Law 8 of the canon says that becoming one is a quagmire, and Law 10 says that living long is hard. Neither has anything to do with being better.

What is a Stealth Wizard?

It is a name that casts the wizard of the canon as a class in a game.
By Law 6, results cannot be seen from outside. That is why it is called "stealth".
Until 2026-11-21 it was called the "Quiet Wizard" (in Japanese, 地味な魔法使い).

Does a class sort people?

No.
A class here is a game metaphor, like a job in a role-playing game. It does not sort people into groups, and it is not something to pin on anyone.
It is not an advanced class, and it is no stronger or better than any other class.

Is the operator a wizard?

This site says nothing about anyone's sexual experience, the operator's included.
The operator does not speak for wizards.

Does one have to be a virgin to become a wizard?

Reading Definition 1 alone, having or lacking sexual experience is not part of what makes a wizard.
Law 1 describes one road to becoming a wizard.
Nowhere does the canon say there is no other road.

Is there a way to tell who is a wizard?

No.
By Law 6, results cannot be seen from outside.
This site does not call any particular person a wizard.

What happens on the thirtieth birthday?

Nowhere does the canon say that anything happens.
Thirty is only a rule of thumb (Law 5), and results cannot be seen from outside (Law 6). Proposition 3 of the Principia reaches the same conclusion by another route.

Is there a concrete example of magic?

None is written down.
By Law 9, writing down specifics brings harm back to the one who wrote them.

What happens after becoming a wizard?

A quagmire (Law 8).
And a long life (Law 10).
Living long is hard, so sleep and water matter.

Does this site ask about sexual experience?

No.
This site collects nothing from anyone. There are no input fields and no cookies.
Scenes for the Field Guide by Visitors are sent in as GitHub issues by those who wish to. That form does not ask about sexual experience either.
