What this site is not

  • This is an entertainment space, separate from the operator's academic work. It is not science.
  • They are not scientific laws. The laws of the canon were written from the author's intuition.
  • It is not medical, psychological, or sexual advice. It judges no one's sexual experience.
  • It does not call any particular person a wizard. Nor is it material for making fun of anyone.
  • This is an in-joke, and some people may find it offensive. Please keep that in mind.
  • It is not elitism. Wizards are not treated as better than other people, or as chosen. Having or lacking sexual experience does not make anyone worth more or less.
  • The operator does not speak for wizards. Nothing here was written on anyone's behalf.
  • It is not a paper. The canon itself says that it "is not a paper".
  • It is not part of the operator's research. Apart from "The scholarly text" below, it is not linked to any research page.

The scholarly text

A scholarly analysis of this urban legend exists apart from this site. That analysis is the authoritative text; this site is reading for fun.

  • Title: 「30歳魔法使い」都市伝説の構造分析 —— クラスチェンジと強化解除の物語的メカニズム (a structural analysis of the "wizard at thirty" urban legend, on the narrative mechanisms of class change and buff removal; in Japanese, version 1.0, 2026-10-03)
  • DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23114131 (Zenodo)
  • Knowledge Commons record: vaa96-ss949
  • An English version 1.1 (The "Wizard at Thirty" Urban Legend: A Structural Analysis) is also on Knowledge Commons and Zenodo. Its DOI is not given here, because it has not been checked.

That text has not been peer reviewed. The canon and the Principia of this site are not part of it.

Who wrote what

  • The canon: a single text written by the operator. This site copies it without changing a word, and the English page is a translation of it.
  • The playful parts: the texts of the home page, the field guide, the FAQ and the playground, and the build of the pages, were written by Claude (a language model made by Anthropic) at the operator's request. Claude is not an author.
  • The rule pages: the terms of use, the privacy policy, requests and contact, the notes on legality, the legal handling of the design, and this page were also written by Claude at the operator's request.
  • The Principia: a version that rebuilds the definitions and laws of the canon in the style of a classical mathematics book, written by Claude at the operator's request. It is not the canon. Until 2026-11-21 it was called the "strict version".
  • The English pages: translations of the Japanese pages, written by Claude at the operator's request. If the two differ, the Japanese page governs.
  • This site says nothing about anyone's sexual experience, the operator's included.

What is collected

Nothing.
There are no input fields, no cookies, and no access analytics. What is pressed in the playground is counted only inside the browser.
The head of every page carries a Google Search Console ownership token, but the pages send nothing to Google with it (privacy policy).
The pages request nothing from any other server.
Vercel hosts the site, and handles the requests it receives under its own rules in order to serve the pages.
