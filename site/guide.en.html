Stealth Wizard Field Guide

図鑑

This is a play. What follows is imagination: the ten laws of the canon applied to scenes from daily life. None of it comes from observation or surveys, nor is it a scientific claim.

Appearance

  • Staff: none. A folding umbrella instead.
  • Hat: none. The umbrella is chosen to suit the weather.
  • Robe: a navy or grey jacket, picked to last for years.
  • Familiar: none. There is one potted plant.
  • Thermos: yes. In Japanese it is a "magic flask", which is the only magic about it.

Spells

The spells of a Stealth Wizard are short, and their effect cannot be seen.

SpellWhat happens
Let me double-checkThe talk pauses once, and there is one mistake fewer
I'll be heading off nowThe wizard goes home. Nothing else happens
It's fineIt becomes fine. That can take a while
Just in caseThere is one more spare

How the ten laws quietly show

LawHow it quietly shows
Law 1 EstablishmentNothing happens on the thirtieth birthday. Nothing happening is the sign of establishment.
Law 2 Many FacesIn meetings the listening face is turned outward; among friends, the talking face. Both belong to the same element.
Law 3 ConservationA word that went unsaid is not thrown away. It grows quietly at the back of a drawer.
Law 4 ReversalBuilt-up frustration one day appears as a knack for noticing other people's frustration.
Law 5 Rule of ThumbSome switch at twenty-seven. Counting years does not tell.
Law 6 ReturnA result melts into someone's vague sense of "that helped, somehow". Sometimes even the wizard cannot see it.
Law 7 Common PracticeThose who have become one do not notice each other. If they do, they say nothing.
Law 8 QuagmireAfter getting into bed, the brain runs one more lap.
Law 9 SecrecyNo specifics are written here. That is because of Law 9.
Law 10 Long LifeA long life. So knees and lower back get good care.

Belongings

  • A spare charger
  • Sticky notes
  • A key of unknown purpose in the coin purse
  • A notebook shown to no one

How to tell one

There is no way to tell.
By Law 6, results cannot be seen from outside, and by Law 7, those who have become one do not say so to each other.
Using this guide to decide that someone nearby is a wizard is outside this play.
